Witness Facility

See designated tests as they run on the floor in Bhiwadi or through coordinated online observation.

Why witness

Observation that builds buyer confidence

When a type test or critical run matters to your customer, witnessing lets a nominated representative follow the agreed sequence without guessing what happened behind closed doors.

On-site witnessing

Attend at H1-958, RIICO Chopanki. Your representative observes the scheduled methods in the laboratory under site safety rules.

  • Best for complex or multi-day campaigns
  • Direct dialogue with lab engineers during holds
  • Photo and note policy agreed before the visit

Online witnessing

Where the method and facility setup allow, observe agreed segments remotely with prior scheduling and identity confirmation.

  • Useful for distributed QA teams
  • Session window locked to the test plan
  • Recording rules confirmed case by case
