Witness Facility

Observe designated tests as they run — on the floor in Bhiwadi or through coordinated online witnessing.

Why witness

Observation that builds buyer confidence

When a type test or critical run matters to your customer, witnessing lets a nominated representative follow the agreed sequence with full transparency.

On-site witnessing

Attend at H1-958, RIICO Chopanki. Your representative observes the scheduled methods under site safety rules.

  • Best for complex or multi-day campaigns
  • Direct dialogue with lab engineers during holds
  • Photo & note policy agreed before the visit

Online witnessing

Where method and facility setup allow, observe agreed segments remotely with prior scheduling and identity confirmation.

  • Useful for distributed QA teams
  • Session window locked to the test plan
  • Recording rules confirmed case by case

How to schedule

1

Request a slot

Share product, standards, dates, and on-site vs online.

2

Confirm the plan

We align capacity, safety briefing, and scope.

3

Attend the run

Witness the agreed steps; deviations documented.

4

Receive the report

Impartial results follow the completed method set.

Confidentiality

Sample identity, constructions, and results are handled under laboratory confidentiality practices. Competitor access is not permitted.
