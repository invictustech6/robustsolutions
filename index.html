Who we are

An independent testing laboratory built on integrity

Robust Testing Solutions Pvt Ltd. is an independent, third-party electro-technical testing laboratory. We provide impartial testing and reporting for electrical cables, conductors, transformers, insulators, smart meters and conduits against national and international standards including IS, IEC, BS, UL, and ASTM. As an ISO 9001:2015 certified and ISO/IEC 17025:2017 accredited laboratory recognized by the Bureau of Indian Standards, we are committed to precision, integrity, and responsive, customer-driven service.
