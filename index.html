Who are we?

Electro-technical testing with integrity

Robust Testing Solutions is an independent third-party electrical testing laboratory in Bhiwadi, Rajasthan.

We test and report on electrical conductors, wires, cables, and conduits against national and international standards including BS, IEC, IS, UL, and ASTM — supporting manufacturers, exporters, and QA teams who need impartial, standards-aligned results.
