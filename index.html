Who are we?

A lab built for clear, impartial results

Robust Testing Solutions is an independent and third-party electrical testing laboratory. We are ISO 9001:2015 certified and ISO 17025:2017 accredited, and recognized by the Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS). We are committed to providing testing and reporting for electrical conductors, wires, cables, and conduits against national and international standards including BS, IEC, IS, UL, and ASTM. We continuously improve the quality of our testing and services without compromising integrity. We are customer-driven and responsive.

How testing works

From sample intake to signed report

A clear path so procurement and QA know what happens inside the lab before, during, and after the test run.

1

Define the scope

Standards family, product type, and whether witnessing is required.

2

Receive and identify

Samples logged, labelled, and held under controlled chain of custody.

3

Execute the method

Tests run to the agreed procedure; witnesses observe when scheduled.

4

Report and release

Results compiled into an impartial report for your compliance file.
