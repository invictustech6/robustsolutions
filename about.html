Who we are

Robust Testing Solutions is an independent and third-party electrical testing laboratory. We are ISO 9001:2015 certified and ISO 17025 accredited, and recognized by the Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS).

Our facility is at H1-958, RIICO Industrial Area Chopanki, Bhiwadi, Rajasthan — serving manufacturers, exporters, and procurement / QA teams who need impartial, standards-aligned reports.

  • Independence and impartiality at the core of every engagement
  • Standards families: BS, IEC, IS, UL, ASTM and related scopes
  • Working hours: Mon–Sat 9:30 AM – 6:30 PM · Sunday OFF
