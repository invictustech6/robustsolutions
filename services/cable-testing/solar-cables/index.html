Solar Cables

Solar / PV DC cables are checked for thermal endurance, UV and ozone resistance, and dielectric performance relevant to photovoltaic plant duty.

Cable Testing

What this pathway covers

  • Thermal endurance / lifetime assessment as specified
  • UV and ozone resistance of sheath compounds
  • Damp-heat and water-immersion dielectric checks
  • Cold impact and bend at low temperature where named

Technical reference

Standards, products & samples

The standards below are those commonly specified for this product category, shown for reference. The exact methods within our accredited scope are confirmed when we quote.

Commonly specified standards

  • EN 50618
  • IEC 62930
  • IS 17293
  • TÜV 2 PfG 1169 as named

Applicable products

  • PV string and array DC cables
  • Solar installation cables up to the named DC voltage

What to send

Representative DC cable samples with cross-section, insulation/sheath compound, and voltage rating.

Scope & turnaround

Turnaround is confirmed at the quoting stage based on the methods and sample count. Testing within our NABL-accredited scope (TC-16290) is identified per enquiry; tests outside the accredited scope can be offered on a non-accredited basis where available.
