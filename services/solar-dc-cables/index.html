Technical reference

Standards, products & samples

The standards below are those commonly specified for this product category, shown for reference. The exact methods within our accredited scope are confirmed when we quote.

Commonly specified standards

  • IEC 62930 — PV DC cables
  • EN 50618 — electric cables for photovoltaic systems
  • Ageing / weathering evaluations per the referenced standard

Applicable products

  • PV string & array DC cables
  • Solar installation cables

What to send

Representative DC cable samples with cross-section, insulation/sheath compound, and voltage rating (e.g. 1.5 kV DC).

Scope & turnaround

Turnaround is confirmed at the quoting stage based on the methods and sample count. Testing within our NABL-accredited scope (TC-10626) is identified per enquiry; tests outside the accredited scope can be offered on a non-accredited basis where available.
